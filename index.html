Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, flexible cancellations, pricing transparency, and our guest guarantee.

Booking with Stackly is designed to be effortless: browse our curated destinations or search hotels by name and region, choose your check-in and check-out dates along with the number of guests, and explore tailored room options.

When you click Estimate & Book on any hotel card, our instant estimator computes the exact total—including all room nights, seasonal discounts, and hospitality taxes. Once confirmed, you receive an instant digital receipt and check-in voucher via email.

We know travel schedules can shift unexpectedly. The vast majority of boutique resorts and city hotels on Stackly feature Free Cancellation up to 48 hours prior to your local 3:00 PM check-in time.

Properties offering this flexibility display a green Free Cancellation badge on their card. Cancellations made within the grace window receive a 100% refund credited back to your original payment card within 3–5 banking days.

We partner directly with boutique properties to ensure you enjoy the most competitive rates available anywhere online. If you find the identical hotel, room class, dates, and cancellation conditions advertised for less on any authorized public site within 24 hours of booking, let us know.

We will immediately match the lower rate and credit an additional $50 travel voucher toward your next stay with Stackly.

Yes, 100% transparent pricing is our standard. Unlike platforms that add unexpected resort and cleaning fees at the final payment step, Stackly displays a comprehensive cost breakdown upfront.

All mandatory municipal tourism levies, cleaning fees, and VAT/sales taxes are calculated right inside the live price estimator so there are zero surprise charges at checkout or upon hotel arrival.

You can discover active seasonal promotions in our Special Offers section. To apply a promo, click Copy Code or manually type the code (such as AUTUMN20 for 20% off or SUMMER15 for 15% off) into the coupon field in the booking estimate modal.

The system validates the code in real-time, displays the exact dollar savings, and applies the discount directly to your estimate.

Click the heart icon on any hotel card to bookmark stays you love. Your saved properties synchronize to your browser's local storage automatically.

Access your collection anytime by clicking the Saved badge in the top navigation bar. From the drawer, you can inspect nightly rates, remove items, or jump straight to reservation planning.

Stackly never charges administrative modification fees. Any change in total cost simply reflects the hotel's seasonal rate differential for the updated dates or room category.

We support all major payment providers: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Discover, as well as selected local payment gateways.

Every transaction is secured using bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption and PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant tokenization. Your card credentials are never stored on plain text servers.
